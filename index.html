Who We Are

A community built on knowledge, character and faith.

J. R. P Islamic Dawat Committee is a community-oriented Islamic initiative focused on spreading authentic Islamic knowledge, encouraging good character, supporting youth development, and organising beneficial activities for the local Muslim community.

Our work is rooted in the Qur'an and authentic Sunnah, carried out with wisdom, sincerity and respect for all.
