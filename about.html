Who We Are

A community-oriented Islamic initiative.

J. R. P Islamic Dawat Committee is a community-focused Islamic initiative that works to spread authentic Islamic knowledge, encourage good character, support youth development, and organise beneficial activities for the local Muslim community.

Our work is grounded in the Qur'an and authentic Sunnah, carried out with wisdom, sincerity, and respect for all. We believe that knowledge, when paired with good character, transforms individuals — and individuals, when united, transform communities.

We strive to be a source of benefit for our neighbourhood: for our youth, for our families, and for every person who seeks to learn and grow.

Our Vision

A community rooted in knowledge and good character.

To see a community where every individual — young and old — has access to authentic Islamic knowledge, is inspired to live by the Qur'an and Sunnah, and contributes positively to the society around them.

Our Mission

What we set out to do every day.

📖

Qur'an & Sunnah

Promote authentic Islamic knowledge based on the Qur'an and authentic Sunnah, with proper references and clarity.

🌱

Youth Development

Help young people understand their purpose, responsibilities, character, and Islamic values.

🤝

Community

Encourage unity, brotherhood, mutual support, and beneficial community initiatives.

🕌

Dawah

Share Islamic knowledge with wisdom, good manners, and sincerity — as taught by the Qur'an and Sunnah.

📚

Education

Organise structured learning sessions, study circles, and quiz programs to encourage lifelong learning.

🕊️

Good Character

Cultivate Islamic character (akhlaq) through role-modelling, reminders, and practical guidance.

Our Objectives

What we aim to achieve.

1. Spread authentic knowledge

Provide the community with clear, well-referenced Islamic knowledge from reliable sources.

2. Nurture the youth

Engage young people with meaningful programs that build faith, character, and confidence.

3. Strengthen community bonds

Organise activities that bring the community closer — through learning, service, and shared purpose.

4. Support mosque initiatives

Assist local masjid efforts in education, organisation, cleanliness, and community service.

5. Encourage good character

Remind ourselves and others to live with honesty, kindness, patience, and humility.

6. Serve the community

Organise beneficial initiatives that benefit Muslims and neighbours alike.

Our Values
